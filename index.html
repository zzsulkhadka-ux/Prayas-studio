About the studio

Built on craft,
tempered by iteration.

Prayas means effort — and that's exactly how we work. Frame by frame, revision by revision, until a shot earns its place in the final cut.

We're a small studio of animators, compositors, and directors who'd rather spend three extra passes on a render than ship something ordinary. Every project moves through the same forge: a rough idea goes in, and something considered, textured and alive comes out.

  • Inspire — we start from a feeling, not a template.
  • Design — every frame is composed on purpose.
  • Discover — the best shot is usually the fifth version.

What we do

Six crafts, one production line.

Motion Graphics & Animation

2D and 3D motion design for titles, explainers, brand films and everything that needs to move with intent.

VFX & Compositing

Seamless effects work — from clean-up plates to full CG integration that holds up in a 4K grade.

3D Modeling & Rendering

Product visualization, environments and characters, built and lit for the screen.

Concept Art & Previs

We visualize the shot before a single frame is rendered, so nobody's guessing on set.

Color Grading & Finishing

Grading and mastering that gives every frame its final mood, from grounded to graphic.

Sound Design & Mix

Original score, sound design and mix — the half of the picture people feel but never see.
